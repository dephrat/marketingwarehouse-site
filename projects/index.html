Projects

Another tall order, delivered on time.

Hockey-stick Muskoka chairs? Check. Several thousand ice buckets for a liquor store promotion? Check. Here's some of what we've made for our clients.

Unicef Canada

Custom-knit toques

We designed and produced these toques right here in Canada, fast enough for Unicef's canvassers to hit the streets during the winter months. Custom Canadian-made toques, mittens and scarves are a status symbol for patriotic customers, and very cost effective too.

Heinz

Ketchup-bottle pool floats

Full body-sized inflatable pool floats in the exact shape of the iconic Heinz Ketchup bottle. The client's agency has repeated the order a couple of times for summer promotions to store managers and the ketchup-eating public. Everyone in the pool!

Porsche Canada

10-day World Roadshow

3D rubber racing patches and driver bags

For the 2013 Porsche Canada World Roadshow, we created high-end 3D rubber racing patches instead of typical embroidery, and used them on ballistic nylon driver bags, German-made apparel and more. We also branded die-cast Porsche Cayenne replicas for the daily event awards.

Snapple

Branded adventure prize packs

A custom t-shirt, high-end High Sierra backpacks and stainless steel bottles, all branded for a Snapple prize pack.

CN Tower

High fidelity pop-up speakers

A CN Tower director wanted a small but highly perceived gift for staff and partners. We custom-sourced these speakers from a trusted offshore electronics firm, created custom retail-style packaging, and delivered on time and well under budget.

Liquor store promotion

Saved the client over $40,000

20,000 ice buckets and 40,000 glasses

We produced 20,000 acrylic ice buckets, 40,000 satin-etched drinking glasses, custom clear RPET boxes, coasters and rimmer tins, and sourced custom salt and pepper grinders. The client saved over $40,000 compared with their previous suppliers. We'll drink to that!

Rogers, via Fuse Marketing

17,000 bars in 1 week

Custom chocolate bars

Fuse Marketing needed 17,000 custom chocolate bars in one week for a Rogers in-store promotion. We turned it around with delicious Canadian-made chocolate, and kept it cool in refrigerated trucks during a heat wave.

2012 Stanley Cup playoffs

Branded in 7 places

Hockey-stick Muskoka chairs

We created a folding version of the iconic Muskoka chair, made from hockey sticks and branded in seven places with the client's full colour logo, as in-bar giveaways for the playoff season. Whoever got one was definitely in the hot seat.
